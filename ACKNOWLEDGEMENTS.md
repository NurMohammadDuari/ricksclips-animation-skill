## Reference material

The pipeline was measured against publicly available Shorts and films. Those
videos, their music and their sound effects belong to their creators and are
**not** redistributed here. Only measurements and written notes are included.
If you believe something in `references/` or `library/` goes too far, open an
issue and it will be reviewed.

## Films made with the pipeline

Thank you to everyone who builds an episode and shares what broke. The
