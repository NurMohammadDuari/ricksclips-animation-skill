# VISUAL STYLE GUIDE — Law 11 production

Style basis: the toolkit's own measured style DNA (`library/style/ricksclips-dna/STYLE_DNA.md` — 12,761 frames measured from RicksClips3000's published films) plus the author's CB_RULES.md. This guide records what the corrected production actually ships, so the next episode matches it.

## World
- **Sets:** hand-built toon sets (shop, home, home_door, shop2) — wood `#8a6a48`, plaster `#e8dcc4`, trim `#7a5a3e`, ground `#cdbfa4`. Dressing is dense but never blocks the acting lane.
- **Palette:** warm earth set palette; goods colors limited to `#f2b13c / #e0453a / #f7f3e2 / #3fae5a / #c94a5f` (mithai jar family).
- **Lights:** day / morning / golden / night presets (film.js LIGHTS). Night = cold blue base + WARM pools at practical bulbs (repair recipe: PointLight `#ffd9a0` at every lamp mesh). The light changes only at story turns.

## Characters
- Toolkit builtin forms (dad/lady/standard) with outfit palettes; one silhouette per character; white capsule head style, thin ink limbs.
- Faces: kit `standard` (calm, auto-blink) is the DEFAULT; meme faces only when motivated — `smug` for the nephew's arrogance, `wail` once at the begging, `sweetSmile` for the manipulation beat. **Never use `cool` (sunglasses) for sincerity.**
- Contact shadow always on; feet on the ground line; no floating.

## Camera
- 24 mm, level horizon, no roll. Whole 0.40 / medium 0.62 / close head 0.52 framing targets. Rest 0–6 frames before the key word; travel on the verb only.
- Eye-level at doors and counters (z ≥ 1.3 m under any overhead).
- Inserts: straight-on, noun centered, at most one full bystander head in frame.

## Editing
- Cuts ONLY on script place/time jumps (measured 0.74 cuts/10 s). Rest median ~1.4 s. One wail, one handshake, one to-camera CTA per film.
- Captions: one Bangla word at a time (Noto Sans Bengali), band 58–76 % of frame height, faces kept above it.

## Reference integrity
- The reference research (RicksClips3000 corpus, 7 films / 12,761 frames) informs MEASURED habits (palette, motion medians, rest ratios) — no individual frames are copied; the film is an original production in the same language.
