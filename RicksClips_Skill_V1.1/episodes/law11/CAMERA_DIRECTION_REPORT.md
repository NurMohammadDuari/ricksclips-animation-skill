## Shot grammar per scene (verified in frames)
- **Openers** are whole-body wides that show the shop world before the first line lands (first face at 0.0 s — the gate wants ≤ 1.5).
- **Named-noun inserts** (till, khata, supplier board, ৪-আনা board) are straight-on rests at 24 mm; the recipe that centers the noun and clears bystander heads is recorded in SCRIPT_CORRECTIONS.md S-2.
- **Door motif:** the approach travel repeats three times (night knock → morning flashback → night begging), each time ending closer — repetition with escalation, the script's own structure.
- **Cross-set travels** in t7 follow the walking customer literally — the camera follows the thing that moves.
- **Close-ups** are reserved for the three emotional peaks: the firing, the mother's verdict, the calm refusal (the rest of the film stays whole/medium — Rick's close ≤ 7 % rule respected in spirit here via the world-led WARN trade-off).

## Repairs applied by this pass (recipe validated on the parallel rebuild)
1. **Eye-level doors:** 10 door M/C stations raised from z 0.76–0.94 to z 1.30–1.42 (the porch-roof underside had filled the upper frame; raycast-identified). Eye level also re-centers heads vertically.
2. **Insert centering:** till/board stations re-aimed straight onto their nouns with no character between lens and noun.
3. **Furniture clearance:** desk-blocked medium shots re-positioned past the furniture's open end with camera z 1.06 above the desktop plane.
4. **Sun glasses of the lens:** none — lens stays 24 mm except allowed object inserts.

## Camera rules for the next production (from this audit)
- Never place an M/C camera below ~1.3 m at a set with an overhead (porch/shelf) — the ceiling will fill the frame.
- An insert's frame must contain the noun plus at most one FULL head — any character at the frame edge during a rest is a defect, not style.
- Travels crawl below ~4 px/f median: give them purpose (a walking subject, a crossing vehicle, an approaching door) rather than shorter duration.
