# Law 11 — খাতা যার, দোকান তার (artist cut)

**Camera idea: "Who holds the khata holds the shop."** The credit ledger (বাকির খাতা) is the
seat of power: Rafi writes it, the uncle hands it to the nephew, the nephew waves it at
customers who only know Rafi, the uncle arrives at the door without it, and Rafi's own khata
opens in the last shot. On every power change the held thing travels toward the lens.

**Signature:** the home doorway crossed three times — the uncle brings the nephew IN with the
khata (proud), the fired Rafi stands inside, and the uncle begs at the same door at night
WITHOUT the khata, only a sweets box held out flat. Tea is poured on the honoured visit,
absent when he begs.

**Light arc (full circle):** morning gold (thriving shop) → interior warm (mother's warning)
→ dusk blue (fired) → golden (new job rising) → night (failing, begging) → bright morning
(payoff: ৪ আনার অংশীদার).
