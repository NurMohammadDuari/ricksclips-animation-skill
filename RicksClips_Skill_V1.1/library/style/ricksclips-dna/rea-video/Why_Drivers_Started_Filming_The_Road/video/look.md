## Camera, lighting and editing

- Framing: shot size medium close-up 100%; camera angle eye level; subject is the in-focus region (no face; shot size is an estimate)
- Camera: moving camera (gimbal / handheld pan) 100%; typical move pull-out
- Lighting: soft frontal light (flat light). Key light frontal (from the camera side; both sides of the subject about equally bright), at subject height; lighting ratio about 1.1:1 (0.2 stops); soft light (almost no lit / shadow edge); colour temperature neutral (about 5000K); subject and background differ in colour temperature: mixed sources (e.g. warm lamp + cool window light / screen); background clearly blurred (shallow depth of field / portrait mode)
- Grade: natural (close to the original colours); normal saturation, normal contrast, normal blacks, overall cool
- Editing: 17.4 cuts per minute, 1.73s average live-action shot; 1 camera set-up(s) / location(s); 0 jump cut(s) and 0 punch-in(s) within the same set-up; inserts (screenshots / screen recordings / cards / stock) take 97%

**Shooting plan for the remake**

1. Framing: mostly medium close-up, eye level
2. Camera: moving camera (gimbal / handheld pan) 100%; typical move pull-out
3. Lighting: soft frontal light (flat light) — a large source from the camera side, almost no shadow on the subject: facing a window, a ring light, or a big softbox behind the camera
4. Editing: 17.4 cuts per minute, 1.73s average live-action shot; 1 camera set-up(s) / location(s); 0 jump cut(s) and 0 punch-in(s) within the same set-up; inserts (screenshots / screen recordings / cards / stock) take 97%

Gear:

- white desk or white cards to bounce; shadows go lighter

- Note: background clearly blurred (shallow depth of field / portrait mode)
