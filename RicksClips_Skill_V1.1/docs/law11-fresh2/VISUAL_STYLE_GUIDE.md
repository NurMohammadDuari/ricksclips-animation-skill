# VISUAL STYLE GUIDE — Law 11 (fresh2) · the house style this production follows

Basis: the toolkit's measured STYLE_DNA (12,761 frames of @RicksClips3000) plus the
2024–2026 reference corpus in `download/ricksclips3000_analysis/` (STYLE_DNA.md + contact
sheets of seven films). Nothing here copies frames; it encodes the measured rules the
rebuild follows.

## World

1. The world is REAL 3D: floors, walls, counters, doors, roofs, vehicles, lamps — with
   toon materials, one sun + ambient per light preset, soft contact shadows. Never a flat
   backdrop. (This film: 5 hand-built set modules, 10 place/light combinations.)
2. Light changes at the turn of the story: night → morning (flashback) → day → cool
   (ten years) → golden → interior → night → morning → golden. 2–3 light kinds minimum;
   this film uses 5 presets across 10 entries; most-used light = 36 % (gate ≤ 75).
3. Night is cold base + WARM practicals: every night interior/exterior hangs real warm
   bulbs (PointLight #ffd9a0) at the lamps — the v1 "club-dark night" is banned.
4. Readable props: glass is pale translucent with copper lids; tills are brass with a slot;
   boards are DARK green with BRIGHT paper and a red stripe (paper must never read as a lit
   window at night).
5. Set dressing discipline: trees and fences never stand in a door path (|x| ≥ 4.4 m here);
   porches have real low roofs and every camera keeps its eye-line at/above the roof line.

## Characters

1. White capsule-head figures, ink #2a1a1f stick limbs, flat fills with ONE light-left
   gradient, 4–6 px ink outlines in head space.
2. Every character carries ONE giant silhouette signature readable at 1/8 size: kaka's
   taqi cap + specs, bhagne's backwards cap, pashi's tall topi, ma's sari bun, rafi's
   quiff, tulu's tufts, khoddor's comb-back + punjabi.
3. Face geometry sits on the rig's own landmarks: eyes hy−18..−34, mouth ≈ hy+14..+20,
   hairline ≈ hy−90 — accessories ON those lines (the v1 chin-glasses / chest-moustache
   bugs are the canonical counter-examples).
4. Faces are meme expressions from the locked sets, chosen by the emotion the line carries;
   a calm line gets `standard`, never a decorative sunglasses face.

## Camera

1. Rest on the noun, travel on the verb — computed framings (whole 0.45 / medium 0.62 /
   close = head width 0.52), 24 mm for people, 40 mm only for object inserts.
2. Cuts only on place/time jumps the script names (≤ 0.9 cuts/10 s); one move carries a
   line; the camera is at rest when the noun is spoken.
3. Travels arc around bodies, never through them; no tilt while resting; no whip unless
   planned.
4. The world leads about a third of the time: place shots and object inserts give the
   counter, the till, the desk, the board, the sign their own beats.

## Captions and sound

1. One spoken Bangla word at a time, heavy grotesque, white with thin ink outline, baseline
   71.5 %, centred — the kit's measured Rick caption style.
2. Effects are LIGHT: glints, sparks, steam, paper, coin, knock — 1–2 per 10 s on action
   nouns only (measured: 1.1/10 s). No star bursts, no ink speed lines.
3. Mood washes mark the emotion beats (15.9/min on this film, gate ≥ 12).

## What this guide does NOT claim

- It does not claim to reproduce Antik Mahmud's exact look — it follows the measured
  structure of his storytelling (noun-driven camera, world-led thirds, light at story
  turns, readable props) inside the toolkit's own drawing system.
- Bengali visemes are approximated with the kit's Latin mapping; the vendored Bengali
  viseme research is not wired into this production yet.
