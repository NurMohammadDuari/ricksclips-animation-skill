t8 55.05–60.1 · t9 60.1–77.3 · t12 77.3–98.25 · t13 98.25–106.7 · t14 106.7–120.7.

DIRECTOR'S REASON:
"Never guess when the source material can be inspected." The audio is the foundation of
scene timing (directive PHASE 7); a direction map that disagrees with the audio is wrong
even when it renders.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
Every cut now lands exactly on the script's own place/time jump, and every word station
arrives at rest 5 frames before its word (gate: word stations not at rest = 0).

## C-2 · The ৩ মাস পর return (sentence 3)

ORIGINAL SCRIPT (direction): "door knock (fx + sound), kaka walks the path, packet held out, close on his scared face."

IDENTIFIED PROBLEM:
The first draft opened the take on a close camera and left no establishing shot, so the
audience could not re-orientate after the time jump.

CORRECTED SCRIPT (direction):
P (place) shot of the courtyard path first (1.0 s), then W on kaka walking, M as he offers
the packet on "প্যাকেট", side C on his scared face on "হাজির" + knock fx + door_knock cue.

DIRECTOR'S REASON:
After every place/time jump the audience needs geography before emotion; Rick's own cuts
re-establish the world first.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
The courtyard reads in one glance: path stones → door → porch bulb → tulsi pot.

## C-3 · The montage sentence (sentence 7)

ORIGINAL SCRIPT (direction): "one long take, board → khata → khoddor → kaka's line → till → walk-out, with a golden light turn at 'সেই কথা'."

IDENTIFIED PROBLEM:
The golden turn would have required an extra cut (cuts/10 s gate) and the light change
landed mid-sentence where the narration does not mark a turn.

CORRECTED SCRIPT (direction):
One day-lit take carries নম্বর → খাতা/খদ্দের → ফেলতেই → কথা (THE line, spot wash + medium
close so the shop jars never block the face); the cold light owns the whole NEXT take
("১০ বছর পর… ফিরলো") where the dismissal actually happens.

DIRECTOR'S REASON:
Light changes at the TURN of the story (the dismissal), not inside a list; the mood washes
carry the gold/spot accents inside the day take.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
Cold blue-grey shop for the ten-years beat, rafi exiting past the door — the same counter
he served for ten years, now without him.

## C-4 · The accusation run (sentence 9 tail)

ORIGINAL SCRIPT (direction): "খদ্দের close → খাতা desk insert → রাফিকে close → চোর medium → মাল wide → কাছে medium — six stations in 11 s."

IDENTIFIED PROBLEM:
প্যাকেট-ভাগ্নেরে, হাতে-খাতা and চোর-মাল are 0.4–1.1 s apart in the audio; six stations forced
teleport-speed travels (peak 164 px/f) and two word stations could not rest on their words.

CORRECTED SCRIPT (direction):
হাতে M (khoddor + bhagne + desk in frame, 0.9 s) → রাফিকে W on bhagne at the desk (4.6 s
rest) inside which the accusation ("চোর"), the walk ("মাল নিতে গেলো") and the payoff
("রাফির কাছে") all play — khoddor walks to rafi and pays INSIDE the quiet wide.

DIRECTOR'S REASON:
The scene's drama is ONE continuous event; cutting six ways broke the geography of who is
where. Rick holds a wide and lets the acting play.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
bhagne at the desk with the red khata, khoddor crossing to rafi, the coin-box cue — all in
one legible wide; the rage face and the walk both read without a single cut.

## C-5 · The begging beat (sentences 10–12)

ORIGINAL SCRIPT (direction): "kaka C 'আসো' front camera → পারে C → রাফি M → যায় C side."

IDENTIFIED PROBLEM:
The front cameras on kaka at the door shoot the BACK of his head (he faces the door), so
the begging played to a faceless figure.

CORRECTED SCRIPT (direction):
প্যাকেট W (walk-in, back view is CORRECT here — the approach), then পারে M side (his face
at last), রাফি M at the door, যায় C side on rafi with kaka wailing in the shot.

DIRECTOR'S REASON:
The back view belongs to the approach; the face belongs to the emotion. The side cameras
put both men and the door between them in one picture — "খালি মানুষের ভরসাটা শিখানো যায় না"
lands with the door literally dividing the frame.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
Night courtyard, warm porch bulb, two figures and a door.
